1. **contradiction**: Two places give different values, rules or descriptions for the same thing.
2. **undefined-term**: The docs use a term in a sense of their own (a capitalized concept, or a word used as if the reader already knows what it means here) and never define it.
3. **decision-status**: An open question is written as if it were decided, or a settled decision as if it were still open.
4. **undated-fact**: A fact that changes over time (a price, a version, a status, a count, an owner, a date) has no source and no as-of date.
5. **unexplained-number**: A number an agent may have to weigh or trade against (a limit, a budget, a threshold) comes with nothing saying where it comes from or whether it is a hard limit.
