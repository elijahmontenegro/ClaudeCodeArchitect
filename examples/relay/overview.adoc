== Overview

Relay sends notifications for other services, by email and push, through one API.
A calling service hands Relay a message and a recipient; Relay owns delivery, retries and the record of what was sent.

=== Scope

* In: email and push delivery, retries, and the delivery record.
* Out: deciding what to send and to whom. That stays with the calling service.

=== Boundaries

Relay is not a marketing tool: it has no campaigns, segments or schedules.
