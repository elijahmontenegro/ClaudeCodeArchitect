[[delivery]]
== Delivery

Relay stores each message it accepts, then makes attempts until one succeeds or retries stop (see <<decisions>>).

=== Retention

// tag::retention[]
Delivery records are kept {retention-days} days, then deleted.
// end::retention[]

=== Limits

Each account can send at most {send-limit}. Over that, Relay rejects the email, and the calling service decides whether to try again later.
